Introduction

My name is Kwame Asante, a final-year student of the Faculty of Information Technology at Ghana Communication Technology University. I am running for the position of SRC President with a clear vision: to build a student union that truly serves every student — regardless of faculty, level, or background.

Over the past three years, I have served as Faculty Representative, Academic Committee Member, and Peer Mentor. These roles gave me a front-row seat to the challenges our students face daily — from inadequate study spaces to delayed academic feedback. I am ready to turn those observations into action.

4 Pillars of My Agenda
01
Academic Excellence

Advocate for better lecturer-to-student ratios, extended library hours, and a structured exam feedback system within 2 weeks of results.

02
Student Welfare

Establish a student welfare fund, negotiate discounted mental health services, and create a 24/7 student emergency helpline.

03
Infrastructure & Tech

Partner with IT faculty to improve campus Wi-Fi coverage, add more computer lab seats, and digitise the SRC complaint system.

04
Transparency & Accountability

Publish monthly SRC financial reports online, hold open town halls every semester, and create an anonymous feedback portal.

100-Day Action Plan
  • Hold a university-wide town hall within the first 2 weeks to gather student priorities.
  • Launch the GCTU Student Welfare Fund with an initial seed from SRC reserves.
  • Negotiate with management for at least 4 additional hours of computer lab access per week.
  • Introduce a digital SRC complaint tracker — every complaint gets a reference number and a response within 5 working days.
  • Organise an inter-faculty sports day to foster unity across all departments.
  • Publish the first transparent SRC budget breakdown and share it with all registered students.
  • Establish a mentorship programme connecting final-year students with juniors in their faculty.
Why Vote Kwame Asante?

I am not running for a title. I am running for results. Unlike promises that fade after election week, I am committing to a publicly tracked action plan. Every promise in this manifesto will have a deadline and a public status update.

I have the experience, the network, and — most importantly — the drive. I know what it feels like to struggle with resources on this campus, and I know what it takes to navigate administration to create real change. I am asking for your vote not just as a student leader, but as a fellow GCTU student who genuinely cares.

"Together, we can make GCTU a place where every student thrives — academically, socially, and personally. Your vote is your voice. Use it with purpose."
